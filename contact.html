Before you write

I contacted you and didn't hear back

It is possible the email was missed. Please send a reminder if you haven't heard back after two weeks. If you still don't hear back, it is likely the email was read but could not be answered, given the volume of inquiries.

For urgent matters please copy our administrative contact, Hailey Emery, at hbemery@stanford.edu.

PhD program applications

I am usually not able to reply to inquiries about PhD applications, given the volume of email. Stanford PhD programs are committee-based, so there is little we can do before your full application is reviewed.

Current Stanford PhD students can work with any professor in the university. If you are already at Stanford and interested in the lab, please get in touch whichever program you are enrolled in.

Collaborations

I am actively looking for collaborations with researchers in academia and industry. Please get in touch if you would like to work together on a project.

Looking to join the lab?

Open positions, what we look for, and how to apply are all on the openings page. Please read it before writing, so your email can address what we ask for there.
